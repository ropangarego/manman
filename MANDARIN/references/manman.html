四声 Tones
The 4 tones of Mandarin
First Tone — Flat ā
妈
High & Level
Hold a high steady pitch. Like singing a long note. māmā = mother.
Second Tone — Rising á
麻
Rising
Rise like asking a question in English. "Really?" Má = hemp.
Third Tone — Dip ǎ
马
Falling-Rising
Dip down then rise. Like saying "oh?" with hesitation. Mǎ = horse.
Fourth Tone — Falling à
骂
Sharply Falling
Drop firmly like giving a command. "Stop!" Mà = to scold.
Neutral Tone — a
吗
Light & Short
Quick, light, unstressed. Often at end of sentences. Ma = question particle.
Memory Trick
妈麻马骂
Mother (1) scolded (4) the horse (3) hemp (2) — one sentence, all 4 tones using the same sound mā.
